How the Robot picks a move
  • Move generator on a 0x88 board: every legal move incl. castling, en passant, promotion.
  • Evaluation: material + piece-square tables (tapered between middlegame and endgame), bishop pair, doubled/isolated/passed pawns, rooks on open files.
  • Search: iterative-deepening negamax with alpha-beta pruning, principal-variation search, transposition table (Zobrist hashing), quiescence search on captures, null-move pruning, late-move reductions, killer & history move ordering, check extensions.
  • It looks as deep as it can in the time you give it and plays the move with the best guaranteed score.

Full-strength Stockfish is ~3500 Elo and will beat it every time; lower Stockfish's skill level for a fair fight.
